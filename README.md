# Departures

Live London departures on your Android home screen, styled like the platform boards.
Tube, Overground, Elizabeth line, DLR, trams and buses.

**Install:** download the APK from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) on your Android phone
(Android 12 or later), open it and tap Install. After that, the app tells you when there's an update.
